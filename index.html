Privacy policy

Stock Average Calculator for Android · Effective 5 November 2026

In short: we collect nothing. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Your numbers never leave your phone.

Data the app uses

The values you type (shares, prices and the amount to invest) and your appearance choice are kept in the app's private storage on your device, so they are still there the next time you open it. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions, including no internet permission.

Deleting your data

Tap Clear on the first screen, or remove all app data in Android Settings › Apps › Stock Average Calculator › Storage. Uninstalling the app also deletes it.

Third parties

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may process data about downloads and crashes under its own privacy policy. The app itself shares no data with third parties.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Questions can be sent to the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play page.
